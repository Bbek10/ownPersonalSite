The UPS was bought to protect against a five-second brownout. It has never seen one. What it did see was a scheduled two-hour outage, which is long enough that the interesting question is not "does it hold" but "does everything shut down in the right order".

Order matters more than runtime

The VMs have to stop before the host, the host before the NAS, and the NAS before the battery gives up. Get that wrong and the battery buys you nothing except a slower version of pulling the plug.

  1. Guests, gracefully, with a timeout
  2. The hypervisor
  3. The NAS, last, once nothing is writing to it

Test it on purpose

Pull the mains lead on a Saturday morning and watch the whole sequence with a stopwatch. Mine took ninety seconds longer than the configured threshold because one VM ignored the shutdown signal and sat there until it was killed.

An untested failover plan is a guess with a battery attached.

Now the runtime threshold is set from the measured number, plus a generous margin, and the test is on the calendar twice a year.
